# Application icon attribution

Papercraft templates and the semantic-palette tools originate from Omarchy:
https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/tree/c0c63402abee6e8335f9bd3c6da85348b9269b11/icons/papercraft

Copyright (c) David Heinemeier Hansson. MIT; the upstream LICENSE is retained here.
The 86 selected templates are unchanged. MirrorOS renders their color placeholders
with its default theme palette. The upstream printer template is deliberately not
included: the existing MirrorOS device icon retains that name.

Two templates declare additional artwork licenses in their retained descriptions:

- `foot.svg`: footprint by Lennard Hofmann, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Original project: https://codeberg.org/dnkl/foot
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Omarchy adapts the footprint into a terminal illustration; MirrorOS applies its
  palette. This adapted Foot artwork remains CC BY-SA 4.0, not MirrorOS GPL.
- `nvim.svg`: Neovim mark by Jason Long, CC BY 3.0.
  Original project: https://github.com/neovim/neovim.github.io/tree/master/logos
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Omarchy adapts the mark into a folded-band illustration; MirrorOS applies its
  palette. The mark retains its CC BY 3.0 attribution.

The license texts accompany this notice. Names and marks identify applications;
no endorsement by their authors is implied.

The eleven templates in `icon-sources/mirroros-apps/` are original MirrorOS
Papercraft-inspired artwork, GPL-3.0-only, not upstream Papercraft templates:
CMakeSetup, LimineSnapperSync, io.github.lgse.Strata, org.gtk.Demo4,
org.gtk.PrintEditor4, org.gtk.WidgetFactory4, org.gtk.gtk4.NodeEditor,
org.pulseaudio.pavucontrol, qv4l2, qvidcap and stoken-gui.
Their rendered SVGs use the same semantic palette roles.
